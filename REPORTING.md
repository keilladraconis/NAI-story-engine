# Reporting Issues — Story Engine

Thanks for taking the time to file a report. The single best thing you can do to help a fix land quickly is to send the story you were working in, with a clear description of what went wrong.

## What to include

1. **What were you doing?** Which stage, which button, what was on the page. _"Drafting Tam's dossier with Workshop Sends on, below two paragraphs of story."_
2. **What did you expect?** _"The draft to appear below the divider."_
3. **What happened?** _"The first sentence was added to my story paragraph above it."_
4. **Can it be reproduced?** _"Every time, after a reload."_

Screenshots of the sidebar, the panel under the page and any toast are useful.

## Your setup

- **NovelAI subscription tier**: Scroll, Tablet or Opus.
- **The story model selected in NovelAI** (Xialong, GLM, Erato…). Workshop Sends draft with it, so drafting problems often depend on it.
- **Story Engine version**, shown on the script in NovelAI's script editor.
- **Phone, tablet or desktop.**

## Send the story file

**Attach or DM your story file to @keilla-draconis.** Story Engine keeps its brief, vignettes, canon and distill records in the story, so the file usually lets the problem be reproduced on the first try.

Export the story from NovelAI's story menu. It includes your text, lorebook, Memory and Author's Note; if any of that is private, prefer a DM.

## Generation Journal

For problems with what Story Engine's own generation wrote (the facilitator, the critic, a fix or rewrite, a distilled entry, keys), turn on **Generation Journal** in the script settings and reload. A **Generation Journal** panel appears under the page. Reproduce the problem, press **Copy**, and paste the result into your report: it holds every prompt and response.

The Journal lives only in memory: it is not saved with the story and is gone after a reload. It does not record Workshop Sends; those are NovelAI's own generations, visible in NovelAI's context viewer.
